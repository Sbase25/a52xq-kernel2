Device configuration for Samsung Galaxy A52 5G
===============================================
The Samsung Galaxy A52/F52 5G (codename a52xq) is a upper mid-range smartphone from Samsung.

It was announced in March 2021 and released in April 2021.

There are also two different variants for International (SM-A526B) and India (SM-E5260).

## Device specifications
Basic    | Spec Sheet
--------:|:----------------------
SoC      | Qualcomm Snapdragon 750G
CPU      | Octa-core (6x1.8ghz Kryo 570 & 2x2.2ghz Cortex A77)
GPU      | Adreno 619
Memory   | 6GB RAM
Storage  | 128GB UFS
Expansion | Expandable up to 1TB via microSD
Shipped Android version | Android 12, OneUI 4.1
Battery  | Li-ion 4500mAh, non-removable, 25W Wired
Display  | Super AMOLED, 120Hz, 6.5 inch, 1080 x 2400 pixels, 20:9 ratio
Camera Back  | 64 MP f/1.8 (wide), 12 MP f/2.2 123˚ (ultrawide), 5 MP f/2.4 (macro)
Camera Front | 32 MP f/2.2 (wide)
Video        | 4K@30fps, LED Flash
Biometrics   | Face Recognition & Fingerprint Sensor (under display, optical)
Sensors      | Gyroscope, Compass, Virtual Proximity Sensor, Accelerometer
